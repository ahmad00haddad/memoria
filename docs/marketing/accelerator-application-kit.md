# Memoria: accelerator / program application kit (2026-11-18)

Facts only. Anything marked `[?]` must come from Ahmad before sending. Never claim traction we do not have.

## 1. One-paragraph pitch (EN)
Memoria is an Arabic-first platform for Jordanian photographers. A photographer gets a public portfolio page, online bookings with deposit, a booking board, contracts with an e-sign link, delivery galleries, verified client reviews, calendar sync, push and WhatsApp notifications. Clients (brides first) find a photographer, see packages, and book with a deposit instead of chasing WhatsApp messages. Revenue: photographer subscriptions, Basic 15 JOD/month (180/year) and Pro 20 JOD/month (200/year).

## 3. Problem and gap
- Jordan: no competitor combines online booking + deposit + verified reviews. Arabia Weddings is a lead form only (73 Jordan vendors, almost no reviews). Arab-region marketplaces Sawerly and Mosawer are defunct. Details: `docs/competitors.md`.
- Photographers lose time and money on WhatsApp bookings, no-shows and unpaid deposits.
